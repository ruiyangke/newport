# Account and Pro

Sign in to Newport to start a free trial or use Newport Pro on this computer. Your account is at the bottom of the sidebar, below **Settings**.

## What Pro includes

Newport Pro unlocks [Remote apps](remote-apps.md), which runs Linux apps on your server in windows on a Mac with Apple silicon or on Windows. Everything else in Newport works without an account or a plan.

## Sign in

1. Choose **Sign in** at the bottom of the sidebar. Your account page opens in your browser.
2. Sign in there and approve this computer.
3. Newport says "Signed in as" and your email. The sidebar shows your name or email.

While Newport waits, the row says **Finish in your browser**. Choose it for:

- **Open the browser again**, if you closed the page.
- **Cancel sign-in**. The close button beside the row also cancels.

A sign-in that isn't finished within 10 minutes times out. Choose **Try again** in the message.

## Start a free trial

The free trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. It runs on the computer that started it.

1. Sign in on the computer where you want to use Remote apps.
2. Select a server and open **Remote apps**.
3. Choose **Start free trial**.

Each account and each computer can start one trial. You can choose **Upgrade to Pro** at any time, without waiting for the trial to end.

## Use the account menu

Choose your name or email at the bottom of the sidebar. The menu shows your plan beside your name, and a line about it, such as how many trial days are left or when Pro ends. It offers:

- **Upgrade to Pro**, on Free or a trial. It opens the billing page of your account in your browser.
- **Update payment**, when your last payment failed.
- **Use Pro on this computer**, when Pro is on your other computers.
- **Manage account**, to open your account page.
- **Sign out**.

After you choose **Upgrade to Pro** or **Update payment** and finish in the browser, Newport notices within a few minutes and says "You're on Newport Pro." Changes you make from **Manage account** show the next time Newport checks: when it starts, once a day, or when you open the account menu.

### Plan tags

- **Free**: no trial or Pro on this computer.
- **Free seat**: your account has Pro, but not on this computer.
- **Trial**: the free trial runs on this computer.
- **Pro**: Newport Pro runs on this computer.

The sidebar shows only **Trial** and **Pro**. The account menu shows every tag.

## Use Pro on several computers

Each computer you sign in on takes a place on your account. Pro runs on the computers that hold your account's Pro seats.

- **Pro is on your other computers**: this computer has a free seat. Choose **Use Pro on this computer**, in the account menu or on the **Remote apps** page, and move a Pro seat here on your account page. The computer that gives up the seat closes its open remote apps.
- **Your trial is on another computer**: a trial can't move. Choose **Upgrade to Pro** to use Pro here.

If your account has no room for another computer when you sign in, the message offers **Manage computers**. It opens your account page.

## When a payment fails or Pro ends

- If a payment fails, Pro keeps running until the date in the account menu. Choose **Update payment**.
- A plan that won't renew shows when it ends. If remote apps are open, Newport says when they will close.
- When Pro ends, Newport says "Newport Pro ended on this computer." Open remote apps close when this computer's license runs out.

## Use Pro offline

Newport checks your plan with your account about once a day. Without internet access, Remote apps keep working until this computer's license runs out. If Newport can't reach your account when fewer than three days are left, it warns you once, so you can connect in time.

## Sign out

1. In the account menu, choose **Sign out**.
2. Newport asks "Sign out of Newport on this computer?" Choose **Sign out**.

Signing out frees this computer's place on your account and removes the saved sign-in from this computer. Open remote apps close. Your servers and settings stay. See [Storage and recovery](storage.md).

If Newport can't reach your account, it signs out on this computer and asks you to sign the computer out on your account page too. If you sign this computer out on your account page, Newport signs out here at its next check.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "The sign-in timed out." or "That sign-in expired." | Choose **Try again**, and finish in the page that opens. |
| Sign-in fails and offers **Manage computers** | Your account has no room for this computer. Choose **Manage computers**, make room on your account page, then sign in again. |
| **Remote apps** says "This computer has already used a free trial." | A computer can start one trial, under any account. Choose **Upgrade to Pro**. |
| **Remote apps** says "This computer's clock looks wrong" | Set the date and time automatically, in System Settings on a Mac or in Settings on Windows, then try again. |
| **Remote apps** says "Newport couldn't check your plan online" | Connect to the internet, then try again. |
| Your plan didn't change after you bought Pro | Open the account menu to check again. |

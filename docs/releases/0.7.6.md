### New

- Updating Needle no longer stops anything on the server. Terminal tabs move to the new version with their shells, running programs and screens, and stay attached.
- Remote apps keep running through an update. Their windows pause for a moment and come back as they were, on the same Mac windows. This works for Wayland and X11 apps, including Chromium-based browsers, Firefox, GTK and Qt apps, with their sound, clipboard and typing.
- Remote apps also survive a crash of the server's display: the apps keep running and their windows come back.
- The old version of Needle is removed from the server as soon as the update installs, instead of staying on disk.

### Improved

- A shell shows in one tab at a time, across all of Newport's windows. A window whose shells are already open in another window starts empty instead of starting a shell you didn't ask for.
- Any terminal tab can close, including the last one, and no new shell starts in its place.

### Fixed

- Windows minimized on the server open in the Dock with their picture, come back with one click, and never open empty.
- A program that was killed no longer leaves the terminal sending mouse and keyboard reports meant for it.
- A tab that attaches again keeps each screen's keyboard modes, and a job brought back with `fg` keeps its modes.

### Updating from 0.7.5

The first update to 0.7.6 can't move what earlier versions started, so it leaves it running as it is. Terminal tabs opened with 0.7.5 or earlier stay on their old version until you close them. Remote apps already running stay on the version that started them until their display ends, when you close all their apps; apps you open after that carry over. From 0.7.6 on, both carry over to every update.

### Before you install

On macOS, you need Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Windows installers aren't code-signed yet. Linux apps need Newport Pro or a free trial.

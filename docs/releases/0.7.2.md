### Fixed

- On NixOS servers where sudo is allowed without a password, port discovery now shows which process listens on each port, including processes of other users. Before, it didn't find sudo there and showed **Processes: Limited · permission denied**.
- Scrolling inside a hover card, such as a connection's details, no longer scrolls the list behind it.
- On NixOS servers, Remote apps now checks for the graphics driver that apps drawing with OpenGL need, such as GTK 4 apps, and says so when it's missing. Before, Newport didn't warn, and those apps failed to start.

### Improved

- On a Mac, when you close a Linux app's last window, its Dock icon now goes away after 1.5 seconds instead of 3.

### Before you install

On macOS, you need Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Windows versions are experimental and not code-signed yet. Linux apps need Newport Pro or a free trial.

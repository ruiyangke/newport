### Fixed

- A Linux app window that stayed in the background for a while, for example on another desktop or behind other windows, no longer turns transparent when you come back to it. It used to come back only after you clicked it or opened another window of the app.
- The same applies to Newport's own windows.

### Before you install

On macOS, you need Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Windows versions are experimental and not code-signed yet. Linux apps need Newport Pro or a free trial, and are available on macOS only.
